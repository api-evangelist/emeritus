# Emeritus

> Emeritus is a global online education company partnering with 80+ premier universities (Wharton, MIT, Columbia, Harvard, Kellogg, UC Berkeley) to deliver outcome-based executive education, professional certificates, and skill-development programs to 500,000+ learners worldwide across individual, enterprise, and university-partnership segments. Backed by Accel and the SoftBank Vision Fund. Emeritus publishes no public developer API.
